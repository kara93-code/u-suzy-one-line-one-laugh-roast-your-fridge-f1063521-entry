Write one line, at most 200 characters, roasting a fridge or a phone. A house judge compares lines two at a time and the hardest-landing roast takes the $3 pool.

Our entry — the phone as a liar, not an appliance:

> My phone has been at 1% for three hours now, like it's lying to my face to keep me calm while it dies in my hand.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
